## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an **editorial-athletic financial cockpit** engineered exclusively for multi-channel marketplace merchants operating across Amazon, eBay, Otto, and Shopify. It replaces bloated, passive enterprise reporting tables with high-cadence, high-density situational awareness. The emotional tone balances the ruthless analytical precision of an automated trading floor with the lean, kinetic tension of a performance sports lab. 

Visual hierarchy rejects decorative clutter, corporate blurs, and neon "tech" cliches in favor of calibrated typographic authority, disciplined data density, and modular kinetic architecture. Every metric, delta, and curve communicates operational health immediately.

### Architectural Philosophy: Surface-Layered Bento
The interface relies on stacked tonal planar depth rather than traditional grid outlines or visual dividers. Boundaries exist through micro-luminance shifts across violet-tinted neutral tiers (Canvas Mist, Well Sunken, Tile Raise, Tile Peak, and Ink Panel), secured exclusively by diffused ambient violet shadows. Asymmetric, weighted tile distributions replace equal-width card layouts to guarantee structural variance, prioritizing mission-critical financial KPIs while supporting nested satellite metrics.

## Colors

The color system operates on strict chromatic economy. Violet drives primary intent, navigation, and interactive priority; Lime acts as an analytical signal reserved for positive delta, profitability, and clean system states.

### Color Rules & Prohibitions
- **Pure Black Ban:** Hex `#000000` is strictly forbidden. Dark anchors, inverted panels, and foundational typography rely on `ink-panel` / `ink-primary` (`#1B1826`), preserving violet-undertone continuity across all luminance levels.
- **Lime Signal Restraint:** `lime-signal` (`#B4DE2C`) is strictly an informational accent. It is never deployed as an expansive background fill, button container, or glow. On light backgrounds requiring accessible text, swap to `lime-deep` (`#7E9E12`).
- **Chromatic Purity:** Grayscale neutral drift is prohibited; all gray values are derived through the violet-tinted spectrum (`#EEEDF3`, `#FAFAFC`, `#E6E4EC`, `#63607A`, `#9C99AD`).
- **Gradient Prohibition:** Text gradients, iridescent fills, and neon drop-shadows are banned. Interactive surfaces remain flat, tactile, and grounded.

## Typography

The type system implements a strict dual-engine schema: an authoritative geometric sans-serif for UI, narrative structure, and interaction, alongside an unyielding technical monospace engine for every numeric datapoint.

### Type Pairing Engine
- **Editorial & Structural:** Geometric sans-serif delivers punchy, athletic headline lockups and dense UI readability without the softness of humanist fonts or the corporate neutrality of Inter. Display styles utilize negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to anchor the top of asymmetric tiles.
- **Financial Monospace Engine:** JetBrains Mono is mandatory for all numbers, currencies (EUR/USD/GBP), percentages, delta values, SKU identifiers, order numbers, timestamps, and graph axis ticks. Tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be active globally across monospace elements to prevent horizontal jitter during real-time data streaming.
- **Eyebrow Labels:** All bento tile category markers utilize `label-eyebrow` in full uppercase with extended letter spacing (`+0.06em`) and `ink-tertiary` tone.
- **Serif Ban:** Serif typography is barred throughout the entire application.

## Layout & Spacing

The interface is built upon an offset asymmetric grid system engineered for high data throughput and spatial rhythm.

### Grid Dynamics
- **Desktop (≥1024px):** 12-column kinetic grid constrained to a maximum width of `1440px`. Bento tiles must avoid equal three-card row splits. Instead, compose via 8/4, 7/5, 4/4/4 with differentiated row spans, or single span-12 hero modules paired with vertical satellite stacks. Gutter spacing defaults to `1.25rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 6-column grid with dynamic tile reflow. Primary KPI blocks span the full 6 columns; auxiliary operational tiles share 3-column pairings.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Strict single-column stack. Bento tiles expand to full width (`100%`) with reduced margins (`1rem`) and dense interior padding (`1rem`). Horizontal scrolling across the global page viewport is banned; expansive financial data grids must scroll within self-contained tile boundaries.

### Spacing Philosophy
Every spatial interval relies on intentional rhythm:
- `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) govern metadata relationships, icon-to-label gaps, and chip padding.
- `space-md` (16px) controls form field separation and baseline KPI clusters.
- `space-lg` (20px) to `space-xl` (28px) defines standard internal padding for bento tiles, ensuring dense metric sets never feel compressed.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial layering in this design system rejects hard 1px outline boundaries and structural borders in favor of a surface-tier depth architecture.

### Surface Hierarchy
Depth is established purely by background tone elevation against the baseline canvas:
1. **Base Ground:** `canvas-mist` (`#EEEDF3`) serves as the recessed bedrock for the entire viewport.
2. **Depressed Tier:** `well-sunken` (`#E6E4EC`) forms recessed areas inside tiles, such as analytical chart wells, data matrix zebra stripes, and sub-metric holding pens.
3. **Standard Tile Tier:** `tile-raise` (`#FAFAFC`) acts as the baseline bento tile fill, floating slightly above `canvas-mist`.
4. **Focused / Peak Tier:** `tile-peak` (`#FFFFFF`) holds primary input fields, focused interactive elements, or priority KPI callouts.
5. **Inverted Command Tier:** `ink-panel` (`#1B1826`) provides absolute contrast for the main Net-Profit KPI tile and the primary navigation rail.

### Ambient Violet Shadow Mechanics
Elevation is supported by a bespoke, low-opacity violet-tinted shadow system. Generic gray or black drop shadows are prohibited:
- **Resting Bento Shadow:** `0 1px 2px rgba(48, 38, 92, 0.05), 0 10px 30px rgba(48, 38, 92, 0.08)`
- **Hover / Active State:** Elements lift `-2px` on the Y-axis, transitioning with spring physics (`stiffness: 100, damping: 20`) to an elevated shadow: `0 4px 6px rgba(48, 38, 92, 0.06), 0 16px 36px rgba(48, 38, 92, 0.12)`.
- **Divider Elimination:** Solid 1px structural borders separating bento tiles are forbidden. Where micro-separation is strictly necessary inside high-density data tables, use `whisper-line` (`rgba(27, 24, 38, 0.06)`).

## Shapes

The interface balances generous, athletic perimeter curves on structural bento surfaces with disciplined compact radii for internal interactive controls.

### Curvature Geometry
- **Standard Bento Tiles:** Fixed at `1.5rem` (24px) border-radius to produce an organic, architectural tile appearance.
- **Hero Anchors & Inverted Panels:** Scaled up to `2rem` (32px) radius, commanding macro layout focus.
- **Buttons & Action Controls:** Set at `0.75rem` (12px) to communicate tactile, clickable precision without pill-shaped distortion.
- **Badges, Tags & Status Pills:** Full pill contour (`9999px`) to immediately isolate semantic and operational state flags from interactive rectangular buttons.
- **Internal Wells & Chart Containers:** Scaled to `1rem` (16px) to nest symmetrically within parent tile contours.

## Components

### Bento Tiles
Primary layout containers. Tiles sit on `tile-raise` (or `ink-panel` for hero highlights) with `1.5rem` curvature and diffuse violet shadow. Tiles must never feature bounding borders. Internal organization features an uppercase `label-eyebrow` in `ink-tertiary`, followed by the metric or chart area, with trailing secondary statistics grouped using `space-md` spacing instead of divider lines.

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `violet-core` background, `ink-on-dark` text, `0.75rem` radius, bold sans typography. Hover darkens to `violet-press`; active presses translate downward by `1px`.
- **Secondary (Ghost):** Fully transparent background with `violet-core` text and no structural border. Hover transitions the container to `violet-wash` (`#ECE8FA`).
- **Tertiary:** Plain `ink-secondary` inline text link with subtle weight-shift on hover.
- **Prohibitions:** Neon button borders, rounded pills for action CTAs, and outer glow halos are banned. Limit view zones to one primary button.

### Metric / KPI Blocks
Composed of three unified layers:
1. Micro-eyebrow label (`label-eyebrow`, `ink-tertiary`).
2. Tabular monospace value (`data-hero` or `data-lg`, `ink-primary`).
3. Delta pill badge: Positive delta features `lime-deep` text on a semi-transparent lime tint; negative delta features `loss-red` text on a soft red tint.
Optional inline sparklines utilize a single continuous stroke of `violet-core` with a `violet-wash` underfill; the current terminal point is rendered with a precise `lime-signal` dot.

### Data Tables
Tables sit nested inside `tile-raise` or `well-sunken` panels. Header rows employ `label-eyebrow` text anchored by a single `whisper-line` underline. Alternating rows utilize subtle `well-sunken` striping. All numeric, currency, and date columns must use `JetBrains Mono` and align strictly to the right edge. Status indicators display an 8px solid colored dot paired with a concise text label (`lime-signal` for Active/Delivered, `warn-amber` for In-Progress/Review, `loss-red` for Cancelled/Disputed).

### Form Inputs & Controls
Form fields are set on `tile-peak` (`#FFFFFF`) with `0.75rem` radius. Labels are positioned above the field in `body-sm` (`ink-secondary`); help text sits below. When active or focused, fields transition to a clean `2px` solid `violet-core` ring with no soft glow. Monetary fields require monospace formatting with integrated leading/trailing currency unit indicators (EUR, USD, GBP). Floating labels are banned.

### Chips & Status Badges
Pill-shaped compact indicators (`9999px` radius) with `0.25rem` vertical and `0.625rem` horizontal padding. The visual hierarchy utilizes monochromatic or semantic tints:
- Profit / High Performance: Soft lime background with high-contrast `lime-deep` typography.
- Neutral Metric: `violet-wash` background with `violet-core` text.
- Critical Warning: Subdued red background with `loss-red` text.
Emojis within chips or anywhere in the dashboard are strictly prohibited.

### Loading & System States
Circular spinning icons are banned throughout the application. Content loading states rely entirely on skeletal shimmer panels precisely matched to the target bento tile dimensions, pulsing gently across `well-sunken` and `canvas-mist` values.
